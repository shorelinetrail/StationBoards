Frequently Asked Questions

What stations are supported?

All 2,500+ National Rail stations in the UK and all London Underground lines. You can change your station anytime through the web interface.

Does it require a subscription?

No subscriptions! One-time purchase includes lifetime access to firmware updates and live data feeds.

How is the data updated?

The board connects to your WiFi and automatically fetches live departure data from National Rail and TFL APIs every minute.

Can I wall-mount it?

Yes! The board has mounting holes on the back and can be easily wall-mounted or placed on a desk stand.

What's the power consumption?

OLED displays are power-efficient with selective pixel illumination. The board uses <1W on average and can run from a USB power bank.

Is there a warranty?

Yes! 1-year warranty covering hardware defects. 30-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied.
